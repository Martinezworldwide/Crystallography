## 🌌 Overview

**Amethyst** is a violet variety of quartz often used in jewelry and spiritual healing. Its name derives from the Greek word *"amethystos"*, meaning "not intoxicated", a reference to the ancient belief that the stone could prevent drunkenness.

---

## 🔮 Properties

- **Color**: Deep violet to light lavender
- **Hardness**: 7 (Mohs scale)
- **Chakra Association**: Crown chakra
- **Energy**: Protective, healing, cleansing

> ✨ *“Amethyst carries the energy of fire and passion, creativity and spirituality.”*

## 🧪 Fun Fact

Amethyst owes its color to irradiation, iron impurities, and the presence of trace elements, making each crystal a unique piece of Earth's art.
